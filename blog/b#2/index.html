I’m so happy that you wanted to reconnect and be friends.

It’s too bad that everything has happened to you.

You’re right it has been very hard for you.

No, I don’t know your friends, I’m sure they are…’incredible’.

Yes, I am not on social media, you have said that’s weird.

I’m sure they will let you out soon.

You are sounding better already.

Your husband and you are back together, he kicked you out, now kicked you in. He won’t le you travel or meet people alone.

That’s really too bad.

It’s too bad you don’t know about our miscarriage, our lost lonely little soul who we couldn’t hold onto no matter how desperately we wanted to hold its little hand and hear its little cries, who left us with a hole we couldn’t name for a room we couldn’t decorate in a house we cannot fill and gravestone that won’t be erected. We dream of this little baby. We hear their feet in the hallways and laugh when we change their diaper. We dream of joy, and warmth and silly cartoons and complaining about messes made by little hands and sticky fingers.

It’s too bad we do that.

It’s too bad we can’t share in how bad it is.

It’s too bad you won’t shut the fuck up.

I’m glad this phone call is over.
